# Dissertation chapters (draft)

This folder holds **draft dissertation chapters** for the COMP702 report on
RoboLearn. They are generated from the project's real artefacts — the
codebase, the [decision log](../developers/decision-log.md), the
[test evidence](../developers/test-evidence.md) and the
[evaluation](../developers/evaluation.md) — so the technical content is
accurate and verifiable.

## How to use these

- The **Design**, **Implementation** and **Conclusion** chapters are
  grounded entirely in the working system; they are ready to adapt to your
  university's structure and voice.
- The **Introduction** and **Background / Literature review** are provided
  as **scaffolds with prompts**, *not* finished prose. They deliberately
  contain no citations: inventing references would be academic misconduct.
  You must add and verify every citation yourself.
- Treat all of this as a *starting draft to edit*, not text to submit
  verbatim. Read every claim and confirm it matches your understanding —
  this is your dissertation, in your words.

## Integrity note

Everything technical here can be checked against the repository at the tag
you are writing about. Where a claim depends on external literature or on the
not-yet-run human study, the text says so explicitly rather than asserting an
unsupported result.
