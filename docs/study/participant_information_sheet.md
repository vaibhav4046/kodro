## Invitation

You are invited to take part in an MSc research study about a robot-simulation
interface. Please read this sheet before deciding. Ask any question you wish.
Taking part is voluntary.

## What is the study about?

The study compares two ways of finding faults in a simulated robot program. One
shows ordinary console output. The other also shows structured results from five
repeatable simulation runs. We are evaluating the interface, not testing your
intelligence or programming ability.

## Why have I been invited?

You have been invited because you are aged 18 or older and have an interest in
computing, engineering or making. Both novices and experienced programmers are
useful to this study.

## What will I do?

You will complete a short practice task followed by three robot-programming
missions. Each mission lasts no more than ten minutes. You may edit the supplied
program and run the simulator. After each mission you will rate your confidence
and the task's difficulty. The whole session should take about 45 to 55 minutes.

No physical robot is involved. The study does not use a webcam, microphone,
keystroke logger or screen recording.

## Are there risks or benefits?

You may experience mild frustration or screen fatigue. You may pause, skip a
question or stop at any time. There is no guaranteed personal benefit, although
you may learn about robot simulation and help improve the interface.

## Do I have to take part?

No. Choosing not to take part has no effect on grades, employment or access to
services. If you take part, you may stop without giving a reason.

## What information will be collected?

The study records a pseudonymous participant code, the mission condition, whether
the mission was corrected, completion time, edit count, collision count, two short
ratings and optional comments. Your name and contact details are not entered in the
outcome dataset. The consent record is stored separately.

## How will my information be protected?

Study files will be stored on encrypted University-managed storage and accessed
only by the researcher and supervisor. Raw data will not be uploaded to an AI
service or made public. Reports will use aggregated results and will not name you.

The proposed retention period is 12 months after the degree is awarded, subject to
the final approved University and departmental policy. Files will then be securely
deleted.

## Can I withdraw my data?

You may request withdrawal until seven days after your session or the stated
dataset-lock date, whichever comes first. Contact the researcher and give only your
participant code. After the data has been de-identified and locked for analysis,
removal may no longer be possible. The date and final process will be confirmed on
the approved version of this sheet.

## How will results be used?

Aggregated results may be included in an MSc dissertation, presentation and project
repository. Anonymous quotations may be used only where they cannot identify you.
